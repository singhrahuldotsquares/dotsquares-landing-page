What is HubSpot Agent Hub?

Agent Hub is HubSpot's agentic AI layer, where AI "agents" carry out end-to-end tasks - such as prospecting, support resolution and data enrichment - using your CRM data. In Breeze Studio, businesses can customise HubSpot's agents or build their own from scratch, no code required. Dotsquares sets these agents up, trains them on your knowledge and processes, and puts governance around them.
